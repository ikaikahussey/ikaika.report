About Ikaika

Decades of collaborative leadership — rooted in Kalihi.

Ikaika brings decades of collaborative leadership and experience to the Hawaiʻi Legislature — as a climate-tech developer, an education budget and policy expert, a writer, and a lifelong community organizer. He lives in Kalihi Valley with his family of five and several pets.

Roots

Ikaika’s family bridges the archipelagos of Hawaiʻi and the Philippines. His maternal grandparents, Ben and Consuelo Lardizabal, came from Tagudin, Ilocos Sur — Ben running Ben’s Barber Shop on Bethel Street for 40 years while Consuelo taught in Kalihi.

His paternal ʻohana traces to Niuliʻi in the Kōhala district; his grandfather Theodore served in WWII and organized the HGEA Retirees Unit. Ikaika earned a Master’s in Political Science from UH Mānoa. Ikaika is also a jazz musician and plays in the Afrobeat band Jamarek.
